## Why

Adding a sibling with no recorded parents currently creates an unknown father only, and hard-codes biological parentage. The researcher may have evidence for an unknown mother or only know that both parents were involved.

## What Changes

- Offer one unknown father, one unknown mother, or two unknown parents when neither sibling has recorded parents.
- Defer creation until confirmed Connect/Create. Link both siblings through the same family and selected parent-child type.
- Preserve existing family choices, validation and EN/RU accessibility, with clear explanatory text.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `sibling-parent-selection`: Explicit shared unknown-parent options for sibling links.

Related: #333. The parent-to-child existing-family choice is separately tracked in #324.

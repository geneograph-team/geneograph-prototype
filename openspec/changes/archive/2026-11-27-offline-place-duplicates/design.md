# Design

Prepare comparison fields once per record per pair enumeration or draft scan. Fold Latin diacritics and NFC-equivalent input; preserve Cyrillic distinctions in literal keys, compare Russian/Ukrainian/Belarusian Cyrillic through reusable letter rules. Unsupported scripts retain literal matching. Levenshtein similarity is bounded to 256 code units for approximate comparisons; exact names remain eligible. Match exact full names/aliases with no known conflict; otherwise require equivalent context with similarity ≥0.88 (≥0.85 after transliteration), or center distance ≤2 km with similarity ≥0.72 and each radius ≤2 km. Transliteration requires at least four characters. Context compares trailing administrative segments: literal/transliterated equality, or cross-script similarity ≥0.85 with at least six characters; same-script mismatches veto and unfamiliar translated contexts are unknown, requiring close positions unless an exact alias supplies equivalent context. Reject center distances beyond 25 km plus at most 5 km uncertainty per record. Radii alone do not establish matches. This is heuristic, not a geographic identity system; unsupported exonyms and ambiguous records may remain separate. Keep existing fingerprint tuple and normalize legacy tuple on comparison; do not derive fingerprints from transliteration or interface language. #455 owns broader all-pairs performance work.

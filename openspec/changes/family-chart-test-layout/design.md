# Design

The existing view selects one of two independent layout producers. Classic continues to call its original solver. The experimental producer receives the same visible projection, builds transient compact partner units and separate ancestor/descendant tidy hierarchies, and attaches remaining branches as coherent groups. Family Chart v0.9.0 (c7d22492dfc3090109cf28c6d4c82b1ee4ffd770) is the reference; the locally adapted D3 hierarchy v3.1.2 tidy-tree core is vendored with its ISC notice. The Family Chart MIT notice is retained with the adaptation. This is not the full upstream library, and no premium features or demo assets are used.

The existing geometry constants, connector builder and renderer are shared. All projected real people and family edges remain; any unsupported disconnected projection is explicitly rejected. Crossings/other diagnostics are surfaced, not claimed to be resolved. The experiment defaults to one card per person. Order each same-generation partnership component by its real adjacency graph. For a path of any length, all true partners are adjacent; orient the principal family using focus/direct-ancestry context, then stable family order. For branching components, keep the principal pair adjacent and distribute other partners outward by their side (single hubs alternate sides). Child subtrees are ordered and shifted toward their own junctions within existing row clearances. For collateral placement in multi-partner blocks, constrain the root to its ordered sibling interval when a feasible gap exists, so unequal numbers of partners do not push a child group across the principal family. Where more than two partners make universal adjacency impossible, experimental-only extended partner routes use separate nested upper tracks with an explicit junction in the gap next to the outer partner. Reserve track height above the row, keep focus controls clear, and attach each child stem to the actual partner path. Preserve separated/divorced line style and all family identities. Classic has no extended-route metadata and uses its original routing and validation behavior. Upper tracks can still cross incoming ancestor paths when every in-law ancestry is visible; disclose those crossings instead of adding gratuitous duplicate people. Arbitrary cyclic/branching partnership graphs are not guaranteed crossing-free. If ordinary routing reports a distinct-family crossing, trace real parent paths from the focus and visible partners, and consider only shared ancestors with at least two distinct children on those paths (at most 24 routed trials and three accepted rounds). Never repeat an ordinary descendant merely because its family connector crosses another. A candidate moves the shared ancestor's outgoing family context, partitioning children of the same canonical family into separate transient visual contexts if necessary. Keep each child's original parent and partner memberships, including the other known parent; existing parentage remains canonical and no artificial missing-parent controls are introduced. If no safe shared-ancestor split improves routing, retain the connected baseline. Reject disconnected occurrence graphs before layout; verify actual family connector coverage and connectivity afterward, and accept only a crossing reduction with no node/edge-node collision or missing link and no increase in the other tracked geometry diagnostics. Counters count visible occurrences of one canonical ID. No upstream automatic duplicate-collapse behavior is used. This is a conservative experimental implementation of #319, not full verification of all #318 branch/side invariants or an exhaustive solution to every possible crossing.

The checkbox is unchecked by default in each project/session and uses the existing navigation/zoom controls. Switching back restores the saved Classic viewport if focus and zoom have not changed; otherwise it recenters. No canonical records are written.

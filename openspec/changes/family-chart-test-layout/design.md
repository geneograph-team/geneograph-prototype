# Design

The existing view selects one of two independent layout producers. Classic continues to call its original solver. The experimental producer receives the same visible projection, builds transient compact partner units and separate ancestor/descendant tidy hierarchies, and attaches remaining branches as coherent groups. Family Chart v0.9.0 (c7d22492dfc3090109cf28c6d4c82b1ee4ffd770) is the reference; the locally adapted D3 hierarchy v3.1.2 tidy-tree core is vendored with its ISC notice. The Family Chart MIT notice is retained with the adaptation. This is not the full upstream library, and no premium features or demo assets are used.

The existing geometry constants, connector builder and renderer are shared. All projected real people and family edges remain; any unsupported disconnected projection is explicitly rejected. Crossings/other diagnostics are surfaced, not claimed to be resolved. The experiment defaults to one card per person. Order each same-generation partnership component by its real adjacency graph. For a path of any length, all true partners are adjacent; orient the principal family using focus/direct-ancestry context, then stable family order. For branching components, keep the principal pair adjacent and distribute other partners outward by their side (single hubs alternate sides). Child subtrees are ordered and shifted toward their own junctions within existing row clearances. An independent linked-hub household planner handles a principal adjacent couple with leaf secondary partners on both sides. For each family it measures the complete visible descendant footprint, recursively packs sibling subtrees, and centers the child attachments on that family's junction. It reserves disjoint full-depth household territories, retains the principal couple's minimum partner gap, and places secondary partners outward as required by their descendant widths. A long adjacent partnership has an explicit inline junction near its secondary partner, just outside the card; nonadjacent partnerships retain parallel inline lanes. The plan is atomic and checked against other occupied rows. For reconvergent, externally attached or otherwise unsupported descendant regions, or where a proposed grouping worsens routing diagnostics, retain the connected general layout rather than severing real links. This is a local independent-household planner, not a full solution for all possible branch graphs. For collateral placement in multi-partner blocks, constrain the root to its ordered sibling interval when a feasible gap exists, so unequal numbers of partners do not push a child group across the principal family. Where more than two partners make universal adjacency impossible, experimental-only extended partner routes stay horizontal inside the generation row. Distinct overlapping partnerships occupy parallel lanes; actual endpoints meet the corresponding card edges and the line is interrupted with clearance at intervening unrelated cards. The logical span and visible segments share one family identity; diagnostics validate the drawn segments. Each child stem attaches to its own visible lane at the gap next to the outer partner. No extra band space or above-card partner tracks are used. Preserve separated/divorced line style and all family identities. Classic has no extended-route metadata and uses its original routing and validation behavior. The parallel-lane convention and readability for very large partner groups must be checked in the browser. Arbitrary cyclic/branching partnership graphs are not guaranteed crossing-free. If ordinary routing reports a distinct-family crossing, trace real parent paths from the focus and visible partners, and consider only shared ancestors with at least two distinct children on those paths (at most 24 routed trials and three accepted rounds). Never repeat an ordinary descendant merely because its family connector crosses another. A candidate moves the shared ancestor's outgoing family context, partitioning children of the same canonical family into separate transient visual contexts if necessary. Keep each child's original parent and partner memberships, including the other known parent; existing parentage remains canonical and no artificial missing-parent controls are introduced. If no safe shared-ancestor split improves routing, retain the connected baseline. Reject disconnected occurrence graphs before layout; verify actual family connector coverage and connectivity afterward, and accept only a crossing reduction with no node/edge-node collision or missing link and no increase in the other tracked geometry diagnostics. Counters count visible occurrences of one canonical ID. No upstream automatic duplicate-collapse behavior is used. This is a conservative experimental implementation of #319, not full verification of all #318 branch/side invariants or an exhaustive solution to every possible crossing.

The checkbox is unchecked by default in each project/session and uses the existing navigation/zoom controls. Switching back restores the saved Classic viewport if focus and zoom have not changed; otherwise it recenters. No canonical records are written.

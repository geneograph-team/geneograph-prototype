## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Match missing place coordinates after confirmation

Following offline preparation, the importer SHALL automatically attempt at most 150 distinct eligible unresolved places per import, prioritized by linked records, after disclosure and confirmation. Matching SHALL run as an internal default import stage with no pre-import settings block or opt-out checkbox. A concise disclosure beside the import action SHALL explain that place names may be sent to the existing providers. Progress and the stop-and-continue action SHALL appear only while matching runs; provider attribution SHALL remain in the result report. Users MAY stop lookup and continue with completed results. Closing/cancelling the import SHALL discard the entire draft. Preserve authored names, existing coordinates, links and invalid/conflicting source evidence. Only unique strongly matched settlement candidates receive coordinates; uncertainty and historical or unsupported context remain unresolved. Final detached validation SHALL precede commit. Network failures SHALL NOT prevent import; report completed and skipped outcomes truthfully.

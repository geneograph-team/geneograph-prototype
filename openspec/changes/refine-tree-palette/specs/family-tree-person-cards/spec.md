# Delta: Family Tree person cards

## ADDED Requirements

### Theme-specific category tones

Each of the twenty-one independently editable category base colors per theme SHALL derive readable overview, intermediate and detailed card tones with preserved hue where chromatic, plus outline and connector tones. Light mode SHALL use bright pastels and dark mode SHALL use luminous colored detailed surfaces with contrasting dark foregrounds, retaining the dark canvas and selected-card treatment. Neutral SHALL default to near-white in light mode and silver in dark mode. Label ink SHALL be derived from the actual overview fill. The fill, outline and connector channels SHALL remain independent. Theme or semantic zoom changes SHALL NOT change slot assignment, stored base colors, geometry or selection. New defaults SHALL apply to missing/invalid values and explicit resets without overwriting stored valid colors.

### Shared reference and descendant color

In Branch mode the configured reference and its evidenced biological direct descendants SHALL share one independent category, unaffected by the preset or collateral toggle. Unrelated partners and ambiguous/unsupported links SHALL remain neutral unless otherwise attributable. Existing ancestry slots and occurrence provenance SHALL be preserved. Explicit father/mother creation SHALL preserve the selected role independently of unknown gender, without guessing unspecified roles or creating conflicting biological role evidence.

# Global settings

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Settings organization

Global settings SHALL expose General, Storage & backup, Diagnostics and About. General SHALL group theme, language and Product tour, which SHALL remain available from Help. Preview-only startup and timezone controls SHALL NOT appear. Storage SHALL communicate unavailable features honestly. About SHALL group Application (version and website) and Legal & credits (privacy, licenses, data providers), retaining version copy and third-party attribution styling. Help center and support SHALL remain available from Help. Diagnostics behavior SHALL remain unchanged.

### Requirement: Responsive content scrolling

At ordinary supported viewport sizes, only the selected content panel SHALL scroll, with a stable dialog, header, navigation and footer. Section changes SHALL retain navigation focus and reset content scroll; same-section refresh SHALL preserve scroll where practical. Very short viewports SHALL provide one reachable fallback scroll owner. Keyboard users SHALL be able to focus and scroll the panel and reach all actions.

# Family Tree

## Provider and identity

Family Tree SHALL use one local ELK 0.12.1 placement provider and one incremental D3-assisted renderer in Vertical and Horizontal. Vertical is the default. Fan is disabled. Geometry errors offer a localized Retry. When valid ELK geometry cannot be represented by the inline router, the view may display native routes with a diagnostic. The app sends only anonymous layout data to the local solver frame.

Every card appearance SHALL resolve to one canonical person. Duplicates are visual occurrences with stable context IDs and one primary occurrence. Detect reconnections through relationship paths, including collateral marriage bridges and incompatible generation contexts. Ordinary partnerships, repeated family records, and legitimate drawable cycles do not by themselves require copies. Preserve all recorded family memberships and roles, including when visual instances partition a family’s children. A repeated person’s visible upstream ancestry has one owner. Offer transfer only to a compatible occurrence when it preserves reachability and generations. An occurrence whose ancestry is shown elsewhere does not acquire an unknown-parent placeholder on that basis.

Selecting a visible card updates the canonical selection. Explicit Focus and choosing from Recently selected people update the focus and center it, with the current compatible focus requiring no repeat solve. Ordinary search and card selection use the existing selection navigation. Add relative anchors to the clicked appearance and its canonical person. Opening, toggling or canceling its dialogs preserves the focus and viewport. A successful relationship change refreshes topology.

## Visibility and settings

The default ancestor, descendant and extended-family settings are 5, 3 and enabled. The supported ancestor range is 0–8 and descendant range is 0–5. Descendant depth is counted independently below the focus and eligible siblings, with siblings admitted at ancestor depth one or higher. At depth two or higher, Show extended family adds eligible aunts and uncles and applies the same descendant budget below each. This toggle does not add siblings of grandparents or older ancestors. Visibility is independent of branch colors. Draw only admitted family memberships. Committed orientation, depths, extended-family visibility and colors persist per project where browser storage is available. Saved camera bookmarks apply only to matching geometry.

### Staged Tree settings sidebar
Tree Settings SHALL offer Visibility, Coloring and Palette sections in the existing settings-sidebar pattern, with persistent Apply and Cancel actions. The Visibility section SHALL use Show extended family for the existing collateral visibility rule. The Palette section SHALL show all sixteen slots, their reference-relative paths and origins, separate enabled/inactive indicators and missing origins, and theme-specific editing and reset controls. The Palette theme/reset toolbar SHALL scroll with the Palette content, while the modal header, navigation and footer remain outside the scrolling panel. There SHALL be no standalone canvas legend. Switching sections SHALL preserve unsaved edits; dismissal SHALL discard them.

The Visibility section SHALL allow changing the actual project default person through the same person selector as Coloring. The selection SHALL be staged until Apply, validated against the current project, and SHALL NOT implicitly change current focus or branch reference. The branch reference field SHALL explain that colors follow that person's ancestors.

Visibility and Coloring SHALL use a consistent compact, left-aligned form layout while Palette retains the full panel width. The modal dimensions SHALL remain stable when switching desktop sections. Visibility SHALL use Default person and Visible generations headings without repeating the default-person field label visually; the field SHALL retain an accessible name. Coloring SHALL distinguish color channels from branch settings, retain the branch reference selector, and explain that collateral coloring does not alter tree visibility. Generation inputs, color channels and branch count SHALL share the same equal-width three-column grid, with the channel row aligned to the person-picker row and dividers. Controls SHALL reduce to fewer columns on narrow screens without horizontal clipping.

The default-person and branch-reference selectors SHALL display a normal search field without a dropdown toggle. Matching people SHALL be offered only for a nonblank query. Clearing the query SHALL close the results; choosing a result SHALL stage the selection and retain the selected-person preview. Existing keyboard selection and removal SHALL remain available.

## Placement and routing

ELK SHALL own initial placement, compound geometry and generation alignment. Real cards align to their assigned generation axis. Birth chronology is a visual input preference after occurrence resolution, with undated/yearless cases last and recorded-order ties. It yields to ancestral placement and ambiguous household constraints. Changing chronology alone SHALL preserve occurrence identities, ancestry ownership and semantic family endpoints. The layout cache key uses both recorded topology and effective visual order.

Principal partnerships are selected relative to focus, prioritizing direct ancestry, proximity and visible children with stable input ties. The native order prefers male left in Vertical and above in Horizontal in applicable principal pairs, and father left/above for missing-parent slots. Secondary partners retain placement flexibility. Unmet ordering preferences generate nonfatal diagnostics. Ordinary partner gaps use a nominal minimum of 40 layout units. Secondary partners retain midpoint-based clearance and clamping. Use at most two ELK solves per uncached request.

Missing-parent placeholders retain shared dimensions. Vertical paired missing slots span the 300-unit ordinary card width including the 40-unit gap in the unconstrained fixture. Horizontal slots are full-size. Invisible missing-ancestral reservations are bounded by visible depth, move with their owner, and create no canonical records, visible occurrences or routes. Vertical placeholder compounds use zero extra side padding, slot-width reservations and retained depth padding. Horizontal keeps its existing compound rules. Native chains may bend.

Guarded refinement first balances eligible ordinary couples between their two distinct known parental households. It then aligns a sole recorded child’s partnership with its parental couple when the partner has no incoming edge in the visible occurrence layout. Visible parents and placeholders anchor that partner. Collateral compaction freezes the established main branch and translates eligible neighboring components rigidly within their order and clearance bounds. Reject adjustments that worsen protected objectives or increase any unrelated geometric conflict or its multiplicity. Reservations participate in the same bounds.

The inline router uses one clear rail corridor per generation band and orders clearance-connected interval components independently, sharing levels where possible. Retain canonical endpoints and membership coverage. Use rounded terminal elbows and straight shared junctions. Separated and divorced connectors preserve the 10/8 dash pattern and midpoint phase (or routed junction for extended relationships). A crossing is visually distinct from a junction. Interleaved endpoints may still require overlapping spans or crossings.

## Lifecycle and rendering

The keyed renderer mounts visible cards, controls and intersecting connectors. Selection, pan, zoom, branch colors, and semantic detail changes SHALL trigger no ELK solve. Superseded and disposed requests SHALL not publish. The active project retains up to eight compatible layouts across orientations in a bounded in-memory LRU. Cache hits reuse geometry with current display records. Leaving Tree or switching projects disposes the active cache. Tree timing operations use the shared [application diagnostics recorder](../application-diagnostics/spec.md), respecting module scope and detail mode. Detailed mode retains existing stage timings, counters and ELK pass fields; Basic mode retains high-level durations and outcomes. Leaving Tree or switching projects disposes the layout cache but SHALL NOT stop or clear global recording.

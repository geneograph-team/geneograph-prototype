# Shared media previews

Archive tile/filmstrip and Albums Grid/List/inspector SHALL share one preview generation slot. The runtime owns source validation, stale-target cancellation, encodes, display-size derivative URLs, LRU storage, mounted-image leases, and disposal. Adapter policy owns source resolution and eligibility, location, layout, diagnostics, and tiers. Portraits share local source validation and keep their specialized crop, face-region, cache and generation policies. Full-resolution viewers continue to use original sources.

Cache identity SHALL use record identity, source and size revision, and tier, without serializing an inline original into a key or DOM attribute. Offscreen mounts may stay warm until resource pressure. Eviction SHALL detach all lease holders before revocation. A source replacement or project/module exit SHALL invalidate obsolete targets. An asynchronous completion SHALL check target and scope before mounting. Supported sources are local blob and image data URLs, same-origin HTTP(S), and files within the direct-file application directory. Unsupported, oversized or failed previews keep their fallback.

Archive retains its folder-local policy. Albums retains compatible derivatives across pages and modes within a project, with limits of 256 cache entries, 16 MiB encoded bytes, 120 mounted preview images, and at most 160 pending cold targets plus 160 warm restores. A frame mounts at most six warm derivatives. Grid detailed card mounts use separate layout policy with a 128-card retained buffer and up to six observer-driven preparations per frame. Focus can request a preview ahead of prefetch. Albums uses alpha-capable WebP at 128 px for List, 384 px for Grid and 512 px for the inspector before a device scale of up to 2. Scaling SHALL preserve the source aspect and SHALL NOT upscale. Guardrails cap source metadata at 48 Mi pixels and 24 MiB, check known inline payload size, and reject oversized decoded dimensions before canvas drawing.

Diagnostics SHALL report measured decode, generation and queue timing, source category, outcomes, mounts and cache limits without including asset locations or claiming browser memory. Archive retains its existing diagnostic field meanings.
